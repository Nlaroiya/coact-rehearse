EXT. ROOFTOP - DAY

BLAIR and JONAH lie on a picnic blanket.

                    JONAH
          That one looks like a dog.

                    BLAIR
          That's not a dog.
               (BLAIR takes a photo of a cloud)
          It's clearly a dinosaur.

                    JONAH
          You always say dinosaur.

(BLAIR takes a photo of a cloud)

                    BLAIR
               (smiling)
          Because it's always a dinosaur.

JONAH: Fine. A dog-dinosaur. (He sits up.) Happy now?

BLAIR: (BLAIR takes a photo of a cloud) Very.
